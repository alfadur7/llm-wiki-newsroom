## Summary

A position paper by Sayash Kapoor and 24 co-authors, posted to arXiv on 2024-02-27, defines open foundation models as those with broadly available model weights — an [[OpenWeights]] framing that cites [[Meta]]'s Llama 2 and Stable Diffusion XL as examples. The abstract names five distinctive properties of such models, among them greater customizability and poor monitoring, that drive both their benefits and their risks. The authors propose a framework for assessing marginal misuse risk and conclude that current research cannot yet characterize that risk against pre-existing technologies. This page is built from the arXiv abstract page only; the full paper's findings are outside the raw's scope.
