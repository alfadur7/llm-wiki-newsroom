## Summary

In this May 29, 2024 blog post, the Mistral AI team announces the Mistral AI Non-Production License (MNPL), a [[ModelLicensing|model license]] that allows use of its technology for non-commercial purposes and research. The company says it created the license because partners building high-margin products on its models do not always contribute to its success, research, and independence. Codestral is the first model released under the MNPL, and Mistral AI says it will keep releasing other models and code under Apache 2.0, running two product families side by side. The post presents the license as a balance between its commitment to "openness principles" in the [[OpenSourceAI]] debate and its need to grow its business. It is a vendor announcement: it does not quote the license terms, and no outside assessment of them is in scope.
