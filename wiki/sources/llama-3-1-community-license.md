## Summary

This is the license text [[Meta]] publishes for Llama 3.1, dated to the model's July 23, 2024 release. It grants a royalty-free, worldwide license to use, modify, and redistribute the Llama materials, which it defines to include trained model weights and training, inference, and fine-tuning code. The grant carries conditions: redistributors must display "Built with Llama," derived models must carry "Llama" in their names, all use must follow a separate Acceptable Use Policy, and licensees with more than 700 million monthly active users must request a license that Meta may refuse. The raw is the license alone. It says nothing on whether the terms meet any open-source definition, and no outside assessment of them is in scope.

## Key Claims

- [fact] [[Meta]] — defines the licensed "Llama 3.1" materials to include trained model weights plus inference-enabling, training-enabling, and fine-tuning-enabling code
- [fact] [[Meta]] — grants a non-exclusive, worldwide, non-transferable, royalty-free limited license to use, reproduce, distribute, copy, modify, and create derivative works of the Llama materials
- [fact] [[Meta]] — requires anyone who distributes the Llama materials or a product containing them to provide a copy of the agreement
- [fact] [[Meta]] — requires distributors to prominently display "Built with Llama" on a related website, user interface, blog post, about page, or product documentation
- [fact] [[Meta]] — requires anyone who uses Llama materials or outputs to create, train, or fine-tune a distributed AI model to put "Llama" at the beginning of that model's name
- [fact] [[Meta]] — requires every distributed copy to carry a "Notice" text file stating that Llama 3.1 is licensed under the Llama 3.1 Community License
- [fact] [[Meta]] — requires all use of the Llama materials to follow a separate Acceptable Use Policy, which the agreement incorporates by reference
- [fact] [[Meta]] — withholds the license from any licensee whose products had more than 700 million monthly active users in the calendar month before the release date, until Meta grants a license at its sole discretion
- [fact] [[Meta]] — provides the Llama materials and their outputs "as is," disclaiming all warranties, including non-infringement and fitness for a particular purpose
- [fact] [[Meta]] — grants no trademark license beyond using the "Llama" mark to meet the naming requirement
- [fact] [[Meta]] — assigns licensees ownership of the derivative works and modifications they make, subject to Meta's ownership of the Llama materials
- [fact] [[Meta]] — terminates a licensee's rights on the date that licensee files an intellectual-property claim against Meta over the Llama materials or their outputs
- [fact] [[Meta]] — may terminate the agreement on any breach, after which the licensee must delete and stop using the Llama materials
- [fact] [[Meta]] — makes California law govern the agreement and gives California courts exclusive jurisdiction over disputes
