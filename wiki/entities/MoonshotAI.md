## Overview

Moonshot AI is the maker of the Kimi models, cited in this wiki as one of the Chinese developers of [[OpenWeights|open-weight]] models. MIT Technology Review reports that it released the open-weight Kimi K2.5, which came close to [[Anthropic]]'s Claude Opus on some early benchmarks at roughly one-seventh of Opus's price ([[whats-next-chinese-open-source-ai]]). Its later model, Kimi K3, released in July 2026, drew most of the attention. Chinmayi Sharma reports that Moonshot announced K3 on July 16, 2026 and released its weights on July 27 ([[open-weight-diplomacy-digital-silk-road]]). Tom Uren, writing before the release, reports Moonshot saying it would release the weights on July 27 and that K3 "still trails the most powerful proprietary models" ([[knives-out-open-weight-ai-models]]). JJ Jasser, writing while K3 was API-only, argues that calling it open source is [[OpenWashing|open-washing]] ([[open-washing-four-criteria]]).

The company is also a named target of [[Distillation|distillation]] accusations. Anthropic reports that a campaign it attributes to Moonshot seemed to route requests directly from the Chinese military, including one asking Claude to assess surveillance footage ([[anthropic-distillation-campaigns-alibaba-moonshot]]). White House science adviser Michael Kratsios wrote that Moonshot had "developed a sophisticated internal platform to conduct large scale distillation against U.S. models," and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said sanctions and Entity List designations "will be on the table" ([[knives-out-open-weight-ai-models]]). None of these sources carries a response from Moonshot to the accusations.

## Key Facts

- **Rankings** — Uren reports K3 ranked third on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, second on the Vals AI index and first on the Frontend Code Arena ([[knives-out-open-weight-ai-models]]); Jasser reports it among the top five on the Artificial Analysis index and first in the Frontend Code Arena ([[open-washing-four-criteria]]).
- **Size** — Stanford HAI describes K3 as having a million-token context window and gives its size as both 2.8 trillion and 2.4 trillion parameters in the same sentence ([[open-weights-not-enough-open-source-science]]).
- **License** — Jasser expected the weights to arrive under a "Modified MIT" license that is not approved by the [[OpenSourceInitiative|Open Source Initiative]] ([[open-washing-four-criteria]]); Sharma reports that the K3 license requires large hosting businesses to negotiate a separate agreement with Moonshot ([[open-weight-diplomacy-digital-silk-road]]).
- **Revenue** — Sharma reports that Moonshot's paid API revenue has reportedly tripled since K3's release ([[open-weight-diplomacy-digital-silk-road]]).
- **Distillation debate** — as reported by Uren, Dean Ball wrote that he did not think K3's performance "can be explained away by distillation," and Nathan Lambert argues that distillation's impact is overstated ([[knives-out-open-weight-ai-models]]).
