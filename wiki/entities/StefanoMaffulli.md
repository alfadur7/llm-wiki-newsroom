## Overview

Stefano Maffulli is the [[OpenSourceInitiative]] (OSI) executive who led the OSI's Open Source AI Definition (OSAID) effort in the sources collected here; the dated sources run 2023–2024. He left the OSI executive-director post in 2025 (per the OSI's September 2025 executive-director-search announcement) and wrote on his own blog in April 2026 that he was joining Grist Labs to lead revenue generation and open source business strategy; neither document is in this corpus. The sources give his OSI title two ways: TechCrunch in June 2024 and LWN.net in October 2024 call him executive director, while TechCrunch's October 2024 report on OSAID 1.0 calls him executive vice president.

In a July 2023 OSI opinion post, Maffulli wrote that the license for [[Meta]]'s Llama 2 is "very plainly not an 'Open Source' license" under the [[OpenSourceDefinition]]. LWN.net reports that in June 2023 he announced the OSI's intent to define [[OpenSourceAI]]. In June 2024 he told TechCrunch that knowing where [[TrainingData]] came from and how it was processed matters more than the plain dataset. Where TechCrunch raised a possible conflict of interest in Meta's sponsorship of the OSI, he said the OSI could give up Meta's money at any time. At the October 2024 release of OSAID 1.0 he framed it as a way to align policymakers and developers and to flag [[OpenWashing]], while conceding the OSI has no enforcement power. LWN.net quotes him rejecting the charge, made by critics including Tom Callaway and the Free Software Foundation, that the OSAID sets the bar too low. He is also listed among the 25 authors of the paper "On the Societal Impact of Open Foundation Models" and, with an OSI affiliation, among the signatories of the Mozilla-hosted Joint Statement on AI Safety and Openness.
