## Overview

Mistral AI is an AI model developer that on May 29, 2024 announced the Mistral AI Non-Production License (MNPL), a [[ModelLicensing|model license]] that, in the company's words, allows its technology to be used "for non-commercial purposes and to support research work." Codestral was the first model released under the MNPL. The company said it would keep releasing models and code under Apache 2.0, running two product families side by side, one under each license. It justified the license by saying that partners building high-margin products on its models do not always contribute to its success, research, and independence, and it described the MNPL as a balance between its "openness principles" and its need to grow its business. The announcement does not quote the license terms. TechCrunch's Kyle Wiggers reports that Mistral's license bars the use of certain models and outputs for commercial ventures.

In the [[OpenSourceAI]] debate, Mistral AI argues that openness in AI is under threat and that the debate is being used to entrench incumbent players. Outside observers place it differently. Alek Tarkowski and Paul Keller of Open Future say Mistral, like OpenAI, first committed to openness and then moved toward increasing closure. In their peer-reviewed 2024 openness survey, Andreas Liesenfeld and Mark Dingemanse rank Mistral among the big commercial players in the lower ranks of the openness table, and they argue that Mistral and [[Meta]] have lowered average openness because smaller players build on their widely available weights. David Evan Harris lists Mistral among the companies releasing what he calls "unsecured" AI systems.

## Key Facts

- Arthur Mensch, Cedric O, and Charles Gorintin appear with a Mistral AI affiliation among the signatories of the Joint Statement on AI Safety and Openness, a statement hosted on a [[Mozilla]] site.
- Mistral appears in the published signatory list of the 2026 open letter "Open Weights and American AI Leadership," which argues against prohibiting [[OpenWeights]] models.
